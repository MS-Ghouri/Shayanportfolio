01. CONTEXT

Project Overview

Vox Autos is an automotive spare-parts e-commerce platform built around product discovery, catalog browsing and online purchasing.

In the automotive aftermarket industry, online shoppers face challenges when sourcing replacement parts: vehicle compatibility is strict, part variants are numerous, and unstructured search leads to incorrect orders. Vox Autos addresses this by establishing an organized product hierarchy where customers can locate OEM and aftermarket parts with confidence.

02. PROBLEM

The E-Commerce Challenge

The development required setting up a high-SKU catalog capable of scaling across multiple automotive brands and mechanical component categories:

  • Taxonomy Complexity: Automotive components must be classified logically across systems (Brakes, Engine, Suspension, Electrical, Lighting) to enable fast browsing.
  • Mobile Buyer Experience: A significant portion of automotive parts buyers browse catalogs directly from mobile phones while inspecting their vehicles, requiring responsive touch navigation and compact card views.
  • Reliable Checkout: The order flow required clear product specification displays so customers can confirm part numbers before completing their purchase.
