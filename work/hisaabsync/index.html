01. OVERVIEW

Project Overview

HisaabSync was conceived and developed as a collaborative university project to address billing transparency and resident management friction in residential housing societies.

In many residential complexes, monthly maintenance collection and utility splits still rely on manual paper ledgers, fragmented messaging groups, and physical cash receipts. This causes accounting discrepancies, delayed verifications, and lack of clarity for residents. HisaabSync provides a structured mobile-first platform where residents and administrators can track financial obligations transparently.

02. PROBLEM

The Problem & Requirements

The application needed to balance two distinct operational viewpoints:

  • Resident Viewpoint: Residents require immediate visibility into their current dues, transparent access to past payment records, and an easy way to submit payment proof (such as bank transfer slips).
  • Society Administration Viewpoint: Management committees need the ability to generate recurring monthly maintenance bills, review and verify resident payment proofs, and maintain accurate society fund records.
  • Controlled Onboarding: Access had to be restricted to legitimate residents of a given society via unique society access codes, preventing unauthorized user registration.
